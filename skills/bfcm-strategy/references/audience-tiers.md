# Segmentation, Send Tiers, and Exclusions

Have these built and checked before October. Segments assembled on Black Friday morning are segments with bugs.

## Customer segments

| Segment | BFCM treatment |
|---|---|
| **Champions / Loyalists (RFM)** | Early access, first sends, new arrivals. Exclude from paid retargeting |
| **VIP / high LTV** | Early access, framed as a reward |
| **Due to replenish** | For consumables: prior buyers approaching their usual reorder window get a dedicated early send before the BFCM noise |
| **Clicked, didn't buy** | Anyone who clicked a BFCM campaign but hasn't ordered. Follow-up send, and a stronger Cyber Monday offer if margin allows |
| **Needs attention / At risk** | One clear CTA, stronger incentive, no full cadence. Sync to Meta in October |
| **BFCM early access list** | New signups from BFCM forms and social. Shorter copy, exclusive framing |

## Engagement send tiers

Recency tiers decide who receives which campaign, which lets volume grow without burning the list.

| Tier | Definition | Gets |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1** | Engaged last 30 days | Every BFCM campaign |
| **Tier 2** | Engaged 31–60 days | Most campaigns, layered in selectively |
| **Tier 3** | Engaged 61–180 days | Launch, Cyber Monday, last chance only |
| **Tier 4** | Engaged 181–365 days | Launch and last chance only, if deliverability is healthy |
| **Tier 5** | Never engaged | Suppress from campaigns |

## Always exclude from promotional sends
- Profiles with an open customer service ticket
- Anyone who purchased in the last 24–48 hours (move them to post-purchase content)
- Unengaged and error/bounce segments

A full segment audit goes beyond this pack; this file covers what BFCM specifically needs.
