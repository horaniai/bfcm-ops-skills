# Campaigns, Creative, and Offers

## BFCM week send plan

Adjust to the brand's sale dates and apply the send tiers from `audience-tiers.md`. Tier 2 and below drop to 1 email/day max.

| Day | Email | SMS |
|---|---|---|
| **2–3 weeks out** | VIP early access invite (consider a longer early window, up to ~2 weeks) | VIP early access (SMS subscribers) |
| **Replenishment window** | Dedicated send to "due to replenish" segment | — |
| **Week before** | Teaser + gift guide / wishlist nudge | — |
| **Early access day** | VIP launch | VIP launch |
| **Thanksgiving (Thu)** | Gratitude note, no discount, or early open for VIPs | Optional, VIP only |
| **Black Friday (Fri)** | AM launch + PM reminder | Launch alert |
| **Sat** | Best sellers / social proof, or a plain-text founder note | — |
| **Sun (Cyber Sunday)** | Cyber Monday preview + clicked-didn't-buy follow-up | Optional early CM access for VIPs |
| **Cyber Monday** | New angle (bundles, tiers, GWP), AM + PM | CM launch + last chance |
| **Last day of sale** | Broadest send of the week, last chance | Last chance |
| **Giving Tuesday** | Cause or brand values, no hard sell | — |
| **December** | Shipping cutoffs, gift cards for late shoppers, Hanukkah if relevant | Shipping cutoff reminder |

**Cyber Monday needs its own angle.** Don't repeat Friday or send "our sale is extended." Use a variation built around a goal, like bundles or a spend threshold to raise AOV.

**Send times**: Everyone sends at the obvious times (midnight, 9am, noon). Test off-peak, and use send-time optimization for early access sends. Engagement in BFCM often peaks in the evening.

## Creative best practices

- **Subject lines**: state the offer and when it ends. Aim for under ~50 characters so they read on mobile. Test in October, not on Black Friday
- **Preview text** adds new information. Never repeat the subject line
- **Plain-text founder note**: one casual, plain-text email from the founder or CEO often outperforms polished designs because it looks different in a crowded inbox
- **Resend to non-clickers** on the engaged segment only, with a new subject line. Use clicks, not opens, since opens are unreliable
- **Mobile first**: live text, not all-image emails. Minimum ~14px body and ~22px headings. Big tap targets
- **Dark mode check**: some clients invert colors, so all-black BF templates can flip. Test in dark mode before sending
- **Countdown timers**: build one master block and clone it into each campaign
- **Product feeds**: best sellers, recently viewed, and AI recommendations instead of static product grids
- **Universal content blocks** for campaign headers and footers so phase changes are one edit

## Offer and margin guidance

- **Lead with VIPs and repeat buyers.** They already know the product, respond to smaller incentives, and cost nothing to acquire. Test the claim against the brand's own last-year split before building the plan around it
- **Spend thresholds and tiered offers** (e.g. X% off over $Y, more off over $Z) lift AOV better than a flat sitewide %
- **Double loyalty points** as an alternative to a deeper discount
- **Longer early access** captures revenue before peak competition, and early VIP windows tend to earn higher revenue per recipient than the core BFCM days. Verify this on the brand's own numbers rather than assuming it
- **Auto-applied discount links** instead of codes customers have to type
- Don't stack flow or popup discounts on top of the sale unless that's deliberate and margin allows it

## Pre-send checklist (every campaign)
- [ ] Segment and send tier correct, recipient count looks right
- [ ] Exclusions applied (open tickets, recent purchasers, unengaged)
- [ ] Every link goes to a live sale page, with UTMs
- [ ] Discount or auto-apply link tested
- [ ] Subject line, preview text, and sender name checked
- [ ] Test send reviewed on mobile and in dark mode
- [ ] Scheduled in the right timezone
