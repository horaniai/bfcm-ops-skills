## Prep timeline

| Window | Focus | Detail in |
|---|---|---|
| **Jul – Aug** | Diagnose: pull last BFCM baseline, audit list and flow health, build engaged/unengaged segments | `audience-tiers.md` |
| **Aug – Oct** | Grow the list: early access forms, SMS consent push to email-only subscribers, social-to-list capture | `forms-and-flows.md` |
| **Sep – Oct** | Infrastructure: RFM segments, send tiers, exclusions, form and flow variants built and scheduled, QA everything. Test subject lines in October | `audience-tiers.md`, `forms-and-flows.md` |
| **Oct – mid Nov** | Deliverability ramp, teaser content, VIP early access 2–3 weeks out | `deliverability.md` |
| **BFCM week** | Execute, monitor daily, adjust | `campaign-sends.md`, `monitoring-and-retention.md` |
| **Dec** | Giving Tuesday, shipping cutoffs, gift cards, post-BFCM retention, full revert | `monitoring-and-retention.md` |

The two windows most teams regret skipping are Jul–Aug diagnosis and Sep–Oct QA. Both are cheap to do early and expensive to fix once the sale is live.

For campaign planning outside the BFCM season, use the `bfcm-schedule` skill.
